An Act providing for tenant representation on the Great Barrington Housing Authority board.
Be it enacted by the Senate and House of Representatives in General Court assembled, and by the authority
of the same, as follows:
Chapter 26 of the acts of 2018 is hereby amended by adding the following subsection:-
(c) Notwithstanding chapter 121B of the General Laws, a single resident of the town of
Sheffield shall be permitted to serve as either an appointed or elected member of the board of the
Great Barrington Housing Authority and such appointment or election shall be subject to all
other provisions of section 5 and section 5A of said chapter 121B.
